Buyer checks: confirm the supplied count, size, material, ink colour, tip and full contents. Decorative barrels do not establish ink colour. Erasable ink is not suitable for permanent records. No child-safe, non-toxic, waterproof, archival, medical or certified precision claim is independently established. Glue and inks need carrier acceptance and suitable safety documentation; magnets and sharp tools need appropriate handling. Keep small parts, staples, magnets, blades and caps away from young children. Wrist supports and laptop stands are not medical treatments.
