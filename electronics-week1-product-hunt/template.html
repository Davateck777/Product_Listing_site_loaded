Read the sourcing notes & adjust the planning calculator

Source price ≠ planning floor. The original observed ¥ amount is retained. A ¥5.20 minimum is applied only to proposal calculations. Prices are observed catalogue starting prices unless a rendered CNY option price is explicitly identified. They are not confirmed selected-SKU checkout quotes.

Published freight scenario. Dechaser’s China–Nigeria guide publishes 20kg+ Lagos door-to-door air freight at $14.88/kg plus ₦1,500/kg clearing. This is a consolidated-shipment allocation, not the rate for an isolated small parcel. Smaller consignments have different published rates. Battery cargo is separately published at $15.50/kg plus ₦1,500/kg, with 15–25 working days transit, versus 7–10 days for normal cargo. Battery final-mile coverage is unspecified: confirm before ordering.

Electronics readiness. No product is confirmed shipping-ready. Check battery MSDS/UN38.3 as requested, forwarder acceptance, Nigeria-compatible plugs and input voltage, receiver/app support and exact supplied kit. Supplier dispatch claims apply only to stated options/quantities; they are not arrival guarantees.

Chargeable weight. Where supplier packed dimensions exist, the model takes max(packed kg, L×W×H ÷ 6000). The divisor is a planning assumption, not a verified Dechaser rule. Missing dimensions mean a weight-only lower bound. Outer cartons, rounding, domestic China transport, insurance, handling, taxes or other charges may increase costs.

Proposal formula. Round up to ₦100: (max(selected/starting ¥, 5.20) × FX + max(conditional freight, 2,000)) × 1.25. Where freight is pending, ₦2,000 is only your requested planning floor—not researched freight. Compare-at is a proposed future reference price using a roughly 10% planned discount, never a historical offer.

Market context. Jumia prices are search-index observations captured 02 Nov 2026; sizes, brands, packs and even construction may differ. Delivery is excluded. No affordability or profit claim follows from these comparisons. Exact name-search links and access evidence are retained.

Image provenance. Chromium inspected public catalogues and saved supplier catalogue HTML, matching each selected main image by its exact1688offer ID when live rankings changed. These are catalogue main images; first position in every detail gallery and selected-colour matching are not verified. No support images.

FX reference: CNY/NGN 201.985895 (cross-rate) and USD/NGN 1,344.52, 02 Nov 2026 observations. Rates change.
