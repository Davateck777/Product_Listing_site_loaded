Freight, FX, buyer safety & research methodology

Published freight: the Dechaser China–Nigeria guide gives normal-goods air freight ofUSD 12.90–20.50/kg plus clearing. This draft uses the conservative 1–10 kg tier ofUSD 20.50/kg +₦1,500/kg clearing. The guide also mentions₦1,500–2,500/kg clearing elsewhere; re-quote the actual service and exclusions. Current rates may change. Chargeable kg is the greater of actual kg and L×W×Hcm÷6000, subject to carrier rules. These are proportional consolidation allocations, NOT isolated-parcel quotes. Minimum/rounded chargeable weights, China transport, extra cartons, repacking, taxes/VAT, insurance and onward delivery beyond Lagos are excluded or unresolved. Normal 7–10 working-day transit is not supplier dispatch or a delivery guarantee.

Packing: only the exact offer's selected packing is used. Five scenarios include plausible supplier piece dimensions; these fields are not independently measured protective export packs. Eight use weight only and are LOWER BOUNDS until packed volume and minimum charges are known. The other 187 remain pending. Examples rejected include a 1×1×1 cm strainer pack and conflicting 300 g/800 g weights;26 g/34 g scraper fields;500 g versus 80–97 g oven-mitt weights; and 6 g/10 g loose-clip fields. Generic weights shared across single/multi-piece options are not assigned to all variants. No Jumia weight or representative sibling's packing is borrowed.

FX: editable USD/NGN 1,350 and CNY/USD 0.15 give CNY/NGN 202.50. These are rounded planning assumptions,not verified current payment rates. Prior retrieved FX references showed a date later than this session's 27 October 2026 and are not adopted as current. Re-quote FX, forwarder acceptance and selected packing before payment. Observed supplier prices remain separate from the¥5.20 and₦2,000 planning floors.

Buyer checks: confirm material, size, temperature limits and full contents. Steel codes, non-magnetic descriptions, Damascus-style patterns and food-grade headlines are supplier claims, not independently verified construction, origin or certification. A slicing knife is not automatically suitable for bones. Use safe blade storage and ask the carrier about sharp kitchen tools. Oven mitts are not certified fireproof protective equipment; moisture and contact time affect heat transfer. Do not put unrated plastic or silicone near flames, boiling water or microwaves. Draining mats, coasters and decorative PVC placemats are not interchangeable with tested hot-pan trivets. No water jug purifies water or automatically proves BPA-free composition.
