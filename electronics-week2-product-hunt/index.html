Batch planning desk

Proposals ≠ source evidence

Draft sale = 1.25 × [max(observed source, source floor ≥ ¥5.20) × CNY FX + max(conditional freight, shipping floor ≥ ₦2,000)], rounded up to ₦100. When freight is pending, the floor is an unverified budgeting placeholder, not researched shipping. Compare-at values and roughly 12%, 10% or 8% reductions are proposals, not former prices or proven savings. Proposals apply to an unconfirmed supplier quoted unit/pack; affordability, landed cost and profit are not established.

Freight, FX, safety & research methodology

Published freight: Dechaser China–Nigeria guide lists normal goods 1–10 kg at USD 20.50/kg plus ₦1,500/kg clearing; other weight tiers differ. We use proportional allocations, not a verified isolated-parcel quote. Chargeable kg is max(supplier kg, L×W×Hcm÷6000), subject to actual carrier rules. Weight-only amounts are LOWER BOUNDS until protected export dimensions and minimum charges are known. No representative's weight is copied to sibling offers. Taxes, VAT, insurance, rounding/minimums, repacking, China transport and onward delivery beyond Lagos are excluded or unresolved. Published normal transit of 7–10 working days is neither supplier dispatch nor guaranteed delivery.

Battery cargo: UPS units, rechargeable radios, portable routers, cordless irons and any supplied cells need the correct carrier service and documentation. Lithium cells may require SDS/UN38.3. A normal-goods rate is not a battery quote. Conflicting, implausible or unmapped supplier packing fields are left pending, not repaired with invented weights.

FX: editable defaults of USD/NGN 1,350 and CNY/USD 0.15 give CNY/NGN 202.50. These are rounded planning scenarios, not verified current payment rates. Retrieved Forbes USD/NGN and CNY/USD pages display 09 November 2026, later than this session's 27 October 2026 date. That date conflict prevents treating their numbers as current. Re-quote FX and freight before any payment.

Buyer checks: confirm connectors, voltage, polarity, plug, size, capacity and complete contents. Travel adapters change plug shape, not voltage; Swiss, Italian and Australian options are for those destinations, not automatically Nigerian sockets. A compatible laptop or TV name is not the accessory manufacturer. No independent authenticity, electrical-safety, surge-protection, battery-runtime or radio-approval certificate is established. Use competent installation for wall mounts and safe mains work. Small tools are not assumed insulated, meters are not assumed mains-rated and hot soldering tips need proper protection.

Supplier evidence: all 200 have reviewed catalogue title/price observations; only 20 representatives have detail-page findings. The remaining 180 do not have inspected selected-option technical sheets. Title-based features are supplier claims, not testing. Starting prices may represent an accessory or a different variant. In particular the mini UPS body is ¥85, not the ¥15 charger-only starting option; the selected enclosure is ¥13 USB3, not the cheaper USB2 option. Exact contents and MOQ require confirmation. Ready-made choices may simplify ordering, but a mainland dispatch badge is not a guarantee; some detail pages allow up to 10 days.

Jumia Nigeria: every record retains an exact English-name search link and its direct-access result. Twenty family-level query themes produced indexed observations; 28 differentiated evidence entries are saved, including a deliberately unassigned advanced-tester context. Reusing a family benchmark is not a separate exact match. Length, power, kit count, port count, brand and capacity often differ. The Konka E-K906 context is out of stock and a different model. Stock counts such as “2 units” or “4 units” are not multipack quantities. No price is silently converted to a per-piece equivalent. Comparator delivery fees are not included.

Main images only: saved public catalogue HTML was inspected in Chromium, associating each offer link with its main img element. Exactly one main image is downloaded per record; there is no support-image grid. This is not live first-gallery or selected-colour verification. Supplier main photos may show several options, props or accessories not included. Brand and image-use permissions still need confirmation. This selection excludes offer URLs used in previous Electronics catalogues; it does not promise every function is new.
