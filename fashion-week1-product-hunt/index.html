Freight, FX, buyer safety & evidence notes

Published freight: Dechaser China–Nigeria guide, normal goods 20kg+ Lagos-door allocation USD14.88/kg plus ₦1,500/kg clearing. Published normal transit 7–10 days is not a dispatch or arrival guarantee. Smaller shipment tiers differ. Packing dimensions use max(actual kg, L×W×H ÷ 6000); 6000 is an assumption, not a confirmed carrier rule. Eight estimates are weight-only lower bounds; bare-accessory weights are not full protective shipping packs. Current carrier acceptance, quote, rounding/minimums, carton allocation, taxes/duties, insurance and China transport remain unresolved.

FX: 02 November 2026 reference USD/NGN1,344.52 × CNY/USD0.150229 = CNY/NGN201.98589508. These are recorded reference conversions, not live payment rates. Editing FX recalculates proposals; original reference freight evidence remains visible.

Consumer checks: confirm size, full contents, material, fasteners and skin compatibility. Gold-tone, pearl-look, diamond-style and leather-look are not authenticated precious materials. UV400 labels alone do not establish tested sun protection; fashion eyewear is not safety equipment. Jewellery is not certified hypoallergenic. Magnetic accessories need carrier review; keep small parts away from young children. No luck, health, slimming or financial benefit is claimed.

Research scope: each of the 200 selected offers has a reviewed public catalogue title and price observation; 20 representatives also have rendered detail/packing findings. Other items do not have verified selected-option technical sheets. Title-based editorial descriptions are not independent product testing. Mainland dispatch badges and estimates apply only to specified options/quantities and must be reconfirmed; plain ready-made options may be simpler than custom work, but none is guaranteed shipping-ready.

Market comparisons: exact-name Jumia links are retained. Competitor prices were extracted from search-index results; variants, pack quantities, materials and capacity can differ substantially. A 100-card or multi-hanger price remains a whole-pack price. No unit conversion or savings claim is implied, especially when source pack quantity is unresolved. The competitor delivery fee is excluded.

Images: real Chromium inspected live catalogue DOMs and saved public catalogue HTML where rankings changed, matching exact offer IDs. Main images only; no support galleries. Supplier main photos may themselves show multiple designs, contents or props. First position in every detail gallery and selected-colour matching are not independently verified. Image-use and design/IP permission still need checking.
