Commercial guardrails

Retail and compare-at amounts are curated proposals , not extracted sale prices or historical prices. Compare-at values are modest planned regular-price variants, chosen below the selected Jumia comparator except for the mouse. Do not display a crossed-out regular price or advertise these discounts until a genuine reference price is substantiated under applicable rules.

Planned discount = (compare-at − proposed retail) ÷ compare-at × 100. Cost check = overridden CNY cost × your exchange rate + overridden shipping. It excludes every unverified fee and is not a profit forecast . Low-ticket items may be uneconomic with ₦5,000 shipping; consider bundles or confirmed consolidated freight rather than artificially lowering the cost.

Brand “Others” is the requested catalog field, not a claim that every supplier is unbranded. G200’s source card shows 鸿熙康; do not remove actual product branding or imply authorization. Validate all variant specifications and image rights before publishing.
